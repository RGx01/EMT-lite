See your Octopus bills, half-hour by half-hour

Bill periods and day charts from your Octopus account, and a heatmap that shows the half-hours Octopus has no reading for.

Find it in your Octopus account under Personal details → API access.

Remembering stores Octopus’s refresh token in this browser, never your key. “Sign out” forgets it.

Your key goes from this browser to api.octopus.energy and nowhere else. This page has no server: it is static files, and its security policy lets it connect only to Octopus. The key is swapped for a short-lived token at once and not kept.

EMT-Lite works out your bills from Octopus’s half-hourly data. They are not your bill and may contain mistakes: your Octopus statement is the one that counts. If something looks wrong, please raise an issue.
